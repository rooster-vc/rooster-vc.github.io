The company

The startup application is a genre.

Accepted companies look more like each other than they look like their markets. The application has a scorecard. The scorecard is public, and it can be learned. Matching it is what decides who gets in.

Rooster-VC reads the pattern and writes to it. You arrive with your egg — a market, a complaint, the company you want to be. You get back the materials that make that idea look fundable, rewritten for whichever program is taking applications.

The egg is the input. The application is the product. Send it before anyone else is awake.
